How MediMatch Works

From request to donation in minutes

01

Post a Request

Receivers post blood needs with urgency level, blood group, and location. Emergency requests trigger instant alerts.

02

Smart Matching

Our engine scans compatible donors nearby using real-time location and blood compatibility matrices.

03

Instant Alerts

Emergency emails reach eligible donors within 50km. Donors see prioritized requests on their dashboard.

04

Life Saved

Donor confirms, donates, and marks the request fulfilled. Every donation is tracked.

Why MediMatch?

Blood shortages cost lives every day — not because donors don't exist, but because they can't be reached in time. MediMatch solves the coordination problem.

  • 🗺 Location-aware matching using Haversine distance
  • 🧬 Full blood compatibility matrix (A, B, AB, O ± Rh)
  • ⚡ Priority queue sorts requests by urgency automatically
  • 📧 Email alerts to all compatible donors in the vicinity
  • 🏥 Hospital inventory management built in
